About RouteWise

Smart Travel Planning Made Simple

Our Mission

RouteWise is dedicated to helping travelers plan efficient routes, compare fuel costs, and optimize their journeys. We believe that smart travel planning should be accessible to everyone.

Key Features

  • Smart Route Planning: Get optimized routes with multiple stops and real-time distance calculation.
  • Fuel Cost Comparison: Compare prices for Petrol, Diesel, and CNG to find the most economical option.
  • Interactive Maps: Visualize your route on an interactive map with actual paths and waypoints.
